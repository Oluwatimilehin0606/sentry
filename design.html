Your private settings file is public

path.env_exposed
What it is
A file called .env on your site can be downloaded by anyone. It usually holds passwords and keys that connect your site to other services.
Why it matters
Attackers scan for this file automatically. With it they could log into your database, email or payment account.
How to fix
Delete the file from your public web folder today, then change every password and key it contained. Ask your web host or developer if you’re not sure where it is.
